✨ Easy Comment

Faster, smarter YouTube comments

About the extension

Easy Comment helps you quickly create and post comments on YouTube videos. Choose a star rating, generate a comment, preview it, and post — or use the button next to Subscribe for a one-click AI comment.

What you can do

  • Local mode — ready-made comments based on your 1–5 star rating
  • Gemini Nano — on-device AI comments matched to the video topic and title language (no API key)
  • Preview & post — review before sending to YouTube
  • In-page button — generate an AI comment beside Subscribe

Open source

Easy Comment is open source. You can explore the code, report issues, or contribute on GitHub.
